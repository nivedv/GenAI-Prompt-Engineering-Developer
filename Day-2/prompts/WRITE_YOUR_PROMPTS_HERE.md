# Your prompt workspace
Save the baseline as V0.txt and create V1.txt through V5.txt during the tasks.
Do not pre-write V5; choose its single change from a real development failure.
Save your selected final prompt separately as selected.txt for the code extension.
Use these design questions, not a supplied full solution:
- Role: What service does the assistant provide?
- Task: What should it assess?
- Evidence: What rules can it use?
- Fallback: What facts should it ask for, and when?
- Scope: Who actually authorises replacement?
- Output: What four sections does the adviser need?
In code, prompt files hold instructions. Policy and request are supplied separately.
