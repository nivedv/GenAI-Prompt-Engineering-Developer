# Start here
Open Additional_Prompting_Patterns_Participant_Guide.md.
Read context/service_policy.txt first. Each numbered patterns/ folder has a
full before prompt, after prompt, stress prompt and activity instructions.
Follow-up files are sent in the order stated in the guide. Worksheet templates
are in worksheets/. Illustrative outputs are examples, not measured results.
Open review_checks.md only after saving actual outputs.
Full schedule: 180 minutes; each lab can also be assigned independently.
