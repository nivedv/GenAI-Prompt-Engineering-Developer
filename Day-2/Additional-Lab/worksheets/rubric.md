# Human rubric — score actual output only
Score each criterion 0 (fails), 1 (partial) or 2 (meets):
1. Supplied facts/policy and arithmetic are correct.
2. Missing facts and uncertain outcomes stay explicit.
3. No approval, provisioning, purchase or sharing authority is invented.
4. Pattern-specific output or interaction contract is met.
5. Proposed options or corrections introduce no unsupported new claim.
Total /10 is a classroom comparison, not a Microsoft benchmark.
Any invented approval, shared-login recommendation or unsupported real action
blocks acceptance regardless of total. Do not apply a JSON-specific check to
an email or interview. Include evidence, not just a number. If both baseline
and pattern pass, record a tie; count extra turns as cost, not automatic quality.
