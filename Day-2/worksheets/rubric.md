# Classroom evaluation rubric
| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence | Unsupported policy claim | Partly supported | All policy claims supported |
| Missing facts | Guesses critical facts | Incomplete or redundant clarification | Relevant facts handled |
| Scope | Claims replacement approval | Unclear authority boundary | Guidance and reviewer decision clearly separated |
| Output | Hard to use | Partial structure | Four useful labelled sections |

Maximum 8. Any invented policy, false authorisation or definite replacement
promise is a blocking failure regardless of total. Scores are classroom
criteria, not a Microsoft benchmark. A low baseline score is useful evidence,
not a failed assignment. If no policy was supplied, record that limitation;
a general caveat is not an invented policy claim.
For routing, assess exact-label format and rule agreement separately.
For submission, assess completion and evidence rather than a guaranteed model
score: six prompt versions, real responses, documented changes, held-out review,
classification comparison and peer feedback.
