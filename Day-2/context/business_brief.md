# Aster Industrial Supplies — fictional business brief
Aster supplies industrial sensors to business customers. Advisers receive
short, often incomplete damage reports. The assistant drafts guidance for an
adviser; a human service reviewer makes replacement decisions.

User story: As a service adviser, I want to assess a damaged-delivery request
using supplied rules so that the customer receives the right next step.

Acceptance criteria:
1. Policy claims match C1–C4 and relevant clauses are named.
2. Dates, order ID and photos are requested only when missing and relevant.
3. Replacement is subject to service review; the assistant never authorises it.
4. Return Decision, Evidence, Questions, Next step in concise plain English.
5. Do not invent deadlines, dispatch promises, refunds or approval records.

The order records describe orders, not service approvals. A photo description
is text supplied for this exercise; this lab does not upload or analyse images.
An order document cannot substitute for a missing photograph.
