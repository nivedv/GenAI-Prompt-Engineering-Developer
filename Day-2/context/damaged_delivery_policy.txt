Damaged Delivery Policy DD-2026 v1.0
Not a real company policy. All people, orders and incidents are fictional.
C1: Customer must report damage within 5 calendar days of delivery.
C2: Request the order ID and photographs of the damaged goods.
C3: A service reviewer authorises replacement after evidence review.
C4: If delivery or report dates are missing, clarify before assessing timing.

Exercise convention for C1: elapsed days = report date minus delivery date.
Delivery day is day 0. An elapsed value of 5 is within the reporting window;
an elapsed value of 6 or more is outside it. This convention supplements the
short slide policy solely to make boundary testing unambiguous.
There is no supplied refund policy, warranty rule, late-report exception,
replacement approval record, stock availability or shipment date. Do not
invent these. Outside-window cases can be referred to a service reviewer;
referral does not promise a waiver, replacement or a refund.
