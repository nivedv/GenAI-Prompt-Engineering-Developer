SYNTHETIC SUPPORT ROUTING RULES
Damage report: first notification of damage or request to open a damage case.
Evidence follow-up: explicitly updates an existing damage case with requested
photos, order information or dates.
Needs clarification: the request stage is unknown or both stages appear.
These labels concern workflow stage, not whether replacement is eligible.
Missing dates do not automatically change a clear first report to Needs clarification.
Return exactly one of the three labels; use the rules, not keyword matching.
